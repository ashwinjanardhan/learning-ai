**Vectors**

A vector is a list of numbers (CS view), 
that's also an arrow with length and direction (Physics view), 
and math anchors this arrow at an origin point (0,0), on a 2D plane (x,y) or a 3D plane (x,y,z)

Physics lets this arrow sit anywhere in space (literally anywhere)

Vectors can be added and multiplied. 

In vector addition, place the first & second arrow on the plane, then place the origin of 2nd arrow on the tip of first arrow;
  The sum is new arrow from the origin to the new endpoint.

Scaling a vector by a number (Multiplying v with a scalar) can stretch or shrink a vector.
  If scalar is negative (-2v), it flips the direction of the vector
  If scalar is positive and bigger than 1 (2v), it stretches the vector
  If scalar is positive and smaller than 1 (0.6v), it shrinks the vector
